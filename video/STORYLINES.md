# Storylines — NotchPrompter how-to (≈45 s, 16:9, music + on-screen text)

The one thing: **read your reel script right under the webcam, and it never shows up in a recording.**
Viewer: a creator who shoots on a Windows laptop and keeps glancing down at their script.

## A — "Eyes on the lens" (picked)
Device: the object is the explanation. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
The whole film lives on one stage: the top edge of a laptop, the webcam lens in the bezel, and the
black island hanging right under it. Every feature is the island morphing (peek, open, countdown,
speed HUD, camera dot), so the viewer learns the app by watching the one object they'll actually use.
- First 2 s: the bezel and lens up top, a script window far down the screen, a dashed sight-line drawing
  from the lens down to it, and the line "Reading your script on camera?"
- Beats: hook (eyes leave the lens) → the script flies up into the notch → reveal "NotchPrompter" →
  hover / click → paste script, updates live → Ctrl+Alt+Space, 3-2-1, scroll → set the pace →
  camera turns on, it opens itself → pull back: your screen vs. your recording (it's gone) → icon end card
- Look: warm light desktop (#E9E6E1), matte black bezel, the app's own pure-black island, Segoe UI.
- Sound: a bright minimal electronic bed (local MusicGen, ~108 bpm); clicks on clicks, key ticks on
  keycaps, a whoosh on the fly-up, a riser into the "never in your recording" payoff.

## B — "Two screens"
Device: life before and after. Form: split screen to one.
The film runs as two panes the whole time — "Your screen" and "Your recording" — and every feature
plays on the left while the right stays clean, until the panes collapse into the download card.
Strong for the privacy point, weaker at teaching each control (half-size UI).

## C — "The keyboard plays it"
Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Giant 3D keycaps fill the frame; each shortcut struck cuts to the island reacting, on the music's
beat. Bold and fast, but it under-explains hover, the editor and the camera auto-open.
